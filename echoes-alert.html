Personal Safety

A future mobile-first pathway for people facing violence, sexual assault, threats or coercion, designed around privacy, survivor choice and verified support.

Humanitarian Response

A separate route for urgent humanitarian needs, with carefully protected reports, trained human review and responsible institutional referrals.

Important: Echoes Alert cannot currently receive emergency reports, send rescue teams or contact authorities for you. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local official emergency services directly.
