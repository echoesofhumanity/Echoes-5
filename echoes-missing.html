Echoes Missing

When someone goes missing, every moment matters — and so does their dignity. We are designing a protected pathway for verified reports, responsible search coordination and safe reunification.

In development · No live missing-person service

Safe Profile

Optional advance preparation for people living with dementia or cognitive disabilities, with carefully restricted contact details and communication guidance.

Report & Reunite

Future verified missing-person reports, private sighting submissions, trained human review and safe coordination with authorized services.

Important: Echoes Missing is not yet able to accept missing-person reports, coordinate searches or notify authorities. If someone is missing, contact the appropriate official police or missing-person service promptly. If there is immediate danger, contact your local emergency services. Do not wait for this platform to become operational.
