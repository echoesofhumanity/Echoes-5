WHY WE EXIST

Human Dignity Must Become a Shared Responsibility

Echoes of Humanity begins with belonging, but belonging alone is not enough. A global home becomes meaningful when the people within it are willing to protect one another.

Our mission is to connect people, knowledge, communities, organizations and responsible technology so compassion can move beyond intention and become practical human action.

◇

Protect the Vulnerable

Stand with children, vulnerable individuals and civilians whose safety, rights or dignity are at risk.

△

Prevent Exploitation & Violence

Build awareness, protection and response around abuse, neglect, violence and human rights violations.

◉

Respond When Humanity Needs Us

Help create coordinated pathways for urgent situations, humanitarian crises and people who cannot safely protect themselves.

◎

Connect Humanity

Bring people, communities, expertise and organizations together around shared human responsibility.

✦

Share Knowledge

Give learning, experience and awareness the power to travel across borders and strengthen people.

☼

Make Goodness Visible

Show that humanity is not defined only by suffering, but also by courage, kindness, solidarity and hope.
