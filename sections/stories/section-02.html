TELL WITH DIGNITY

We Do Not Own Other People's Stories

Human storytelling carries responsibility. A powerful story is not permission to expose someone, simplify their life or turn vulnerability into spectacle.

Dignity Before Drama

Human value comes before emotional manipulation, shocking imagery or the pressure to attract attention.

Consent & Safety

Publication should consider informed consent, privacy, vulnerability and whether telling the story could create harm.

Context & Verification

Meaningful stories need enough evidence and context to avoid turning assumptions into facts.

Human Voice

Whenever possible, people should be represented as participants in their own story rather than objects described by others.

Responsible Storytelling

Academy can help contributors learn consent, context, privacy, verification and human-centered storytelling.

Many Ways to Tell

A story may later become an article, interview, photograph, audio piece, documentary or another responsible media form.

From Discovery to Understanding

01

Discover

A human act, person, community or idea is brought forward.

02

Listen

The people involved are approached with care so the story begins with understanding rather than assumption.

03

Verify

Relevant facts, context and claims are reviewed before the story is presented as true.

04

Tell

The story is shaped without stripping away complexity, dignity or human voice.

05

Recognize

Some stories may receive additional visibility through the Echoes recognition system.

06

Remember

The story can remain part of humanity's growing memory instead of disappearing with the feed.
