Stories Are Not Only About Suffering

The world is not defined only by suffering. Stories should also preserve laughter, friendship, creativity, love, discovery, cultural beauty, recovery, ordinary kindness and the moments that make life worth sharing.

We tell difficult truths without allowing pain to become humanity's only portrait.
