From a Human Act to an Echo

Echoes is not a competition. It is a path for recognizing human value and carrying meaningful stories farther.

01

Discover

A person, action, community or idea is noticed because it carries something worth sharing.

02

Tell the Story

The human story behind the act is given context, voice and space to be understood.

03

Recognize

Selected stories are honoured as an Echo of the Month without turning goodness into a ranking.

04

Honour

Stories with lasting resonance can become part of the annual Humanity Honours.
