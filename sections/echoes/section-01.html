Compassion

Human beings choosing to care when another person needs kindness, presence or understanding.

△

Courage

People who act with humanity when doing so requires strength, integrity or personal resolve.

◈

Protection

Actions that defend children, vulnerable people, communities or human dignity from harm.

∞

Solidarity

People standing beside one another and turning belonging into practical support.

✦

Creativity

Art, invention and imagination used to connect, heal, illuminate or create human possibility.

◇

Knowledge

People sharing learning, discovery or expertise in ways that strengthen others.

⌁

Peace

Acts that build understanding, reconciliation and human connection across division.

◎

Community

People solving problems together and creating value that no individual could create alone.

↗

Hope

Stories that remind us that difficult circumstances do not have the final word.

◌

Everyday Humanity

Quiet acts that may never become headlines but still change another person's day, dignity or life.

You do not have to change the world to become an Echo. Sometimes changing one person's day is enough to remind us who we are.
