The Partnership Standard

♥

Human Dignity

People and communities come before institutional visibility or commercial advantage.

◎

Transparency

Purpose, roles, material conditions and resource relationships should be understandable and accountable.

◉

Independence

No partner owns Echoes' governance, editorial voice or humanitarian judgment.

⌁

Shared Responsibility

Each participant should understand what it is responsible for and what it is not.

∞

Long-Term Human Value

We seek relationships capable of creating durable value beyond short campaigns or visibility.

How Partnership Works

01

Connect

Introduce the organization, capability, idea or area of shared interest.

02

Understand

Clarify the human need, goals, capabilities, expectations, conditions and possible risks.

03

Design Together

Define roles, safeguards, responsibilities, resources and the intended human value.

04

Agree Transparently

Make material commitments and conditions clear before collaboration begins.

05

Act

Carry out agreed work with appropriate local participation and professional responsibility.

06

Learn & Continue

Review what created value, what did not and whether the partnership should continue, change or end.
