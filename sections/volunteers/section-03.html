FROM WILLINGNESS TO RESPONSIBLE PARTICIPATION

How Volunteering Should Work

01 — Discover

Find the areas of Echoes where your interests, abilities and available time may genuinely fit.

02 — Introduce Yourself

Share what you can contribute, what you hope to learn and the level of commitment you can realistically make.

03 — Prepare

Complete the orientation, learning or role-specific preparation appropriate to the work.

04 — Connect

Join the people and communities involved with clarity about your role and responsibilities.

05 — Contribute

Do useful work reliably, respectfully and within the boundaries of your role.

06 — Learn & Grow

Reflect, receive feedback, share what you learned and strengthen future participation.

Care Requires Standards

Respect

Every person and community retains dignity, agency and the right to be treated as more than a cause.

Safeguarding

Protection of children and vulnerable people comes before a volunteer's desire to participate.

Responsibility

Good intentions do not replace reliability, appropriate conduct or accountability.

Collaboration

Listen to local knowledge, respect professional roles and understand that meaningful work is shared work.

Learning

Remain open to preparation, feedback and the possibility that another person may know better.

Long-Term Thinking

Contribute in ways that strengthen people and communities rather than creating dependency on the volunteer.
