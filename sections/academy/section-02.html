△

◌

♥

↗

Project Readiness

Certificates With Meaning

Academy can recognize completed learning without confusing participation with professional licensure. Echoes certificates should clearly state what was learned and verified, while accredited or legally regulated qualifications must remain explicitly distinct unless delivered with an appropriately accredited partner.

Learning Certificates

Recognize completion of defined Echoes learning programs and make learning visible to the participant.

Volunteer Qualifications

Record preparation completed for particular categories of responsible participation without claiming expertise beyond the training provided.

Professional Certificate Programs

Develop future advanced programs with experts, institutions and appropriate accredited partners where relevant.
