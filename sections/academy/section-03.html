What Humanity Learns Should Not Disappear

Academy is also a place for knowledge to return to the community. Educators, practitioners, researchers, professionals, creators and people with meaningful lived experience can help useful knowledge travel.

Contribution should follow a responsible pathway so that openness does not become misinformation.

01

Contribute Knowledge

Bring a lesson, skill, resource, experience or field insight that may create human value.

02

Review & Verify

Use appropriate expertise, evidence and editorial responsibility to examine what will be taught.

03

Adapt & Translate

Make knowledge understandable and capable of travelling across languages, cultures and levels of experience.

04

Publish & Teach

Turn verified knowledge into useful learning experiences, guides, conversations and resources.

05

Apply

Allow learning to inform projects, volunteering, humanitarian action and everyday human choices.

06

Improve

Bring experience back into the Academy so knowledge can grow instead of becoming static.

Humanity Knowledge Library

The Learning Passport

As the citizen system develops, Academy learning can become part of a person's Echoes profile: completed learning, verified preparation, relevant skills and readiness for appropriate volunteer roles.

It should never become a popularity score or competition. Its purpose is trust: helping people understand what someone has learned and what responsibilities they may be prepared to carry.

Learn. Qualify. Contribute. Teach.
