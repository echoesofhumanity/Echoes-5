RECOGNITION · NOT COMPETITION

This Is Not a Prize for the Best Human Being

Humanity cannot be ranked. Compassion has no leaderboard. Courage has no universal score. Echo of the Month exists to give additional visibility to a story whose human meaning deserves to travel farther.

Human Value

The story reflects compassion, courage, protection, solidarity, creativity, knowledge, peace, community, hope or everyday humanity.

Human Story

Recognition belongs to a story with enough context to understand the people and meaning behind the moment.

Verified Context

Material facts and relevant claims should be reviewed before a story receives wider recognition.

Dignity & Safety

Visibility must never come at the cost of consent, privacy, protection or human dignity.

Lasting Meaning

The story should offer something worth carrying into humanity's growing memory.

An Echo is not a winner. An Echo is a human story we choose not to let disappear.
