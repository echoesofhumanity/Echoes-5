HOW MONTHLY RECOGNITION WORKS

From Discovery to Recognition

Monthly recognition should emerge from the same responsible Echoes process — not from popularity contests, paid promotion or the loudest social-media reaction.

01

Discover & Nominate

A person, community, human act, idea or creative contribution is brought into the Echoes discovery process.

02

Review

The story is considered for relevance, context, human value and whether Echoes can represent it responsibly.

03

Verify

Relevant facts and claims are checked before additional visibility is given.

04

Consent & Safety

Privacy, vulnerability, consent and possible consequences of wider exposure are considered.

05

Understand the Story

Recognition is connected to the deeper human story rather than a simplified heroic image.

06

Recognize

The story receives focused monthly visibility as an invitation to see humanity, not as a declaration of superiority.

07

Preserve

The recognized story remains part of the Humanity Archive rather than disappearing when the month ends.

No Popularity Contest

Public attention may help Echoes discover stories, but likes, follower counts, celebrity, nationality, wealth or campaign size should never determine human worth.

Recognition is editorial and human responsibility — not a scoreboard.
