Trust Before Attention

Echoes will distinguish reporting from interpretation. News, analysis, explainers, opinion, human stories, documentaries and public-information alerts should be clearly identified so audiences know what they are receiving.

Important reporting should be able to show what is known, what remains uncertain, the sources behind material claims, when the work was updated and meaningful corrections when they are required.

A Human Being Is Never Content

Children, vulnerable people, victims and communities in crisis must never become material for spectacle. Consent, privacy, safety, context and dignity come before distribution.

We will not compete for attention. We will earn trust.
