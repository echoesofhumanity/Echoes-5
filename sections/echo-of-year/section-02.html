FROM RECOGNITION TO REMEMBRANCE

How a Yearly Honour Takes Shape

Annual honour should grow from the same responsible system used throughout Echoes. No popularity vote can determine the value of a human life or action.

No Vote Can Measure Human Worth

Audience response can help a story travel and can reveal what resonates with communities, but follower counts, likes, celebrity, wealth, nationality or organized campaigns must never become a measure of human value.

The honour exists to preserve meaning, not manufacture status.
