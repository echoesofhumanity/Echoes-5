FROM RECOGNITION TO REMEMBRANCE

How a Yearly Honour Takes Shape

Annual honour should grow from the same responsible system used throughout Echoes. No popularity vote can determine the value of a human life or action.

01

Discover

Human acts and stories are found through communities, contributors, Media and the wider Echoes network.

02

Understand

The person, context and meaning behind the Echo are given space through responsible storytelling.

03

Recognize

Monthly recognition gives selected stories additional visibility without declaring them superior to others.

04

Revisit & Verify

Before annual honour, relevant facts, developments and context can be reviewed again.

05

Respect Consent & Safety

Continued visibility should still be appropriate for the people whose lives are represented.

06

Honour

A story receives lasting annual visibility because its human meaning deserves to continue travelling.

07

Remember

The story becomes part of the Humanity Archive and the long memory of Echoes.

No Vote Can Measure Human Worth

Audience response can help a story travel and can reveal what resonates with communities, but follower counts, likes, celebrity, wealth, nationality or organized campaigns must never become a measure of human value.

The honour exists to preserve meaning, not manufacture status.
