ANNUAL HONOUR · NOT A FINAL ROUND

Humanity Is Not a Tournament

Echo of the Year does not ask which person was the best, which act was the greatest or which story defeated the others. It asks a different question: which human stories should we carry forward so their meaning is not lost to time?

Lasting Resonance

A story continues to speak beyond the month in which it first received focused recognition.

Human Depth

The honour remains connected to the real person, community and context behind the recognized moment.

Human Values

The story carries compassion, courage, protection, solidarity, creativity, knowledge, peace, community, hope or everyday humanity.

Dignity Over Prestige

Annual visibility must respect consent, privacy and safety rather than turning recognition into celebrity.

Memory

The purpose of the honour is to preserve human meaning and make it discoverable for the future.

Echo of the Year is a continuation of memory, not the final round of a competition.
