COMMUNICATION IS A RESPONSIBILITY

A Human Being Is on the Other Side of Every Message

Echoes should treat communication with the same dignity, safety and transparency expected throughout the rest of the ecosystem.

Privacy

Personal information should be collected only when useful, protected appropriately and not treated as a commodity.

Respect

Every person deserves dignity regardless of whether Echoes can accept an idea, partnership, proposal or request.

Transparency

People should be able to understand what a communication channel does and what happens to information they provide.

Safety

Sensitive humanitarian, safeguarding or vulnerable-person information requires greater care than an ordinary enquiry.

Consent

Submitting a story or contacting Echoes should not automatically become permission for public publication.

Human Connection

Technology can route communication, but it should not erase listening, judgment or human understanding.

Contact Connects the Ecosystem

A conversation can become knowledge, a partnership, a project, a story, responsible media, volunteer participation or simply an answer. Contact exists to help that first connection find its proper place.

Listen first. Understand the purpose. Connect the right people. Protect the human being.
