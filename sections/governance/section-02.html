THE HUMAN VALUE ECONOMY

We Do Not Build the Mission Around Donations

Echoes of Humanity does not accept public donations. The model is designed to create sustainable resources through its own economic ecosystem and through eligible institutional or public funding aligned with the mission.

Marketplace, merchandise and media can create economic value. Public bodies, institutions and organizations may support eligible work through grants or funds. Those resources can then support the operation of the Echoes ecosystem and humanitarian work in the field.

Marketplace

Human skills, knowledge, creativity and meaningful work can generate value within the shared ecosystem.

Merchandise

Purpose-aligned products can contribute to the economic sustainability of the wider system.

Media

Responsible media activity can become both an amplifier of the mission and an economic contributor to its sustainability.

Institutional & Public Funding

Eligible grants and funds may support defined work when their source, purpose and conditions are compatible with Echoes principles.

Voluntary Service and Paid Economic Work Are Different

Citizens and contributors may volunteer their time in one part of Echoes and create legitimate economic value in another. Payment for real work, products, services or creative contribution is not the same as receiving a salary for belonging to the movement or serving on its board.

The distinction must remain visible: what was voluntary, what was paid, why payment was made and under which economic activity it arose.
