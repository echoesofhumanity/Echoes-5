HOW ECHOES IS GOVERNED

Authority Must Never Hide Behind the Mission

Echoes of Humanity is designed around a 25-member governing board. Governance exists to protect the mission, establish responsibility and make major decisions accountable — not to create a privileged class within the community.

Board service is unpaid. Board membership itself does not create a salary or private entitlement to the resources of Echoes.

As the governance structure is formally populated, this space is intended to publish each board member's identity, role, relevant experience, term information and appropriate conflict-of-interest disclosures rather than asking the community to trust an invisible leadership structure.

25-Member Governance

A defined governing body with visible roles and shared responsibility for protecting the purpose of Echoes.

Unpaid Board Service

Governance responsibility is not itself a salaried position within the Echoes model.

Conflict Disclosure

Governance decisions and economic participation must be separated when personal interests could affect judgment.

Public Accountability

Important institutional information should be understandable, traceable and open to appropriate scrutiny.
