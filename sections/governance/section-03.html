THE TRANSPARENCY LEDGER

Every Resource Should Have a Story We Can Explain

Echoes is designed to develop a public transparency system that connects resources with purpose. As financial activity becomes operational, the goal is to make material flows understandable rather than burying them inside annual statements alone.

Source

Where did the resource come from: Marketplace, merchandise, media, a public body, institution or another approved source?

Purpose

Was the resource unrestricted within the mission or provided for a defined program, project or activity?

Use

What operational or humanitarian purpose received the resource?

Result

What work was completed, what human value was created and what was learned?

Conditions

What material restrictions, obligations or relationships accompanied institutional support?

Accountability

Who held responsibility for the decision, and were relevant conflicts identified and managed?

Support Must Never Purchase Control

Financial support should not buy governance authority, editorial influence or control over humanitarian decisions. Where a board member, contributor, partner or related party has an economic interest in a decision, that interest should be disclosed and handled through a defined conflict-of-interest process.

From Transparency to Participation

Citizens, volunteers, creators, partners and supporters should be able to see how their contribution fits into the larger system. Echoes becomes stronger when participation is visible without turning generosity, volunteer work or human service into a competition.

People create value. Value creates resources. Resources enable action. Action creates human impact. Transparency brings that impact back to the people.
