OUR DECLARATION

Before Every Difference, There Is a Human Being

We believe every human life has value.

We believe dignity is not something a person must earn before receiving compassion, protection or respect.

We believe no child should grow up believing the world has forgotten them, and no vulnerable person should disappear simply because their voice is difficult to hear.

We believe borders can define countries, but they must never define the limits of human solidarity.

We believe compassion is not weakness. It is the courage to let another person's reality matter to us.

And we believe every person can become an echo of hope in the life of someone else.

Human Dignity

Every person is worthy of respect, safety, voice and the possibility of hope.

Protection

Children, vulnerable people and civilians must never become invisible when protection is most needed.

Solidarity

Humanity becomes stronger when we refuse to leave one another alone with suffering.

Belonging

No passport, nationality, language, religion or identity determines who is welcome in our common human home.
