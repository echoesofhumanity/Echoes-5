## Foundational culture
The council is a family of service founded on love, compassion, justice, human dignity, mutual respect and practical solidarity. All 25 council members, including the two co-chairs, hold exactly one institutional vote. Co-chairs facilitate and represent by delegation; neither has a veto. Participation is volunteer-based. Any modest expense reimbursement or limited operational compensation must be separately documented, approved transparently, legally reviewed, and must not be presented as salary or guaranteed remuneration. The platform's up-to-3% operating allocation is not an automatic personal entitlement.

## Institutional vs operational authority
Only the 25-member council decides institution-wide policy, constitution, consolidated budgets and strategic commitments. Cluster teams (salkımlar) make delegated operational decisions within approved scope, budget and safeguards; they can present recommendations but do not vote on council matters. Child safety, emergency response, sensitive data and financial transactions require verified professional safeguards before real-world operation.

## Council meeting chamber integration: V11-B
- Every seat gets a role card, scope and named cluster (initially synthetic only).
- Agenda items identify the proposing cluster, responsible council seat and consulted seats.
- Invited cluster representatives can speak, submit reports and make delegated operational decisions outside the council vote.
- A proposal has an immutable synthetic identifier; each of the 25 synthetic seats can have at most one current ballot per proposal. A change replaces, never adds to, that seat's current ballot. Audit trail design must distinguish change history from tally.
- No real authentication, quorum verification, binding votes, sensitive records or personal reimbursements in a public GitHub Pages pilot.
- Real operations require verified identities, protected backend, access control, consent, logs, conflict-of-interest handling and jurisdictional review.
