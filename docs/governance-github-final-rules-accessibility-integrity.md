## A. Institutional governance rules — proposed, not enacted
1. Exactly 25 equal council seats, including two equal co-chairs. Each verified eligible seat has one vote. No co-chair veto, casting vote or unilateral policy/budget authority.
2. The council reserves constitution, institutional strategy and policy, consolidated budget, material commitments, delegation rules, appointments and amendments.
3. Each seat stewards a portfolio and its operational cluster. Within approved written mandate, spending limits, safety and conflict controls, the cluster may decide ordinary operations without a council vote.
4. Cluster coordinators, experts, implementers, interpreters and invited guests do not obtain a council vote from their operational or meeting role.
5. The co-chairs facilitate external/internal coordination and meeting administration, but technical moderation is never institutional voting power.
6. Operational tasks follow offer -> accept -> work -> submit -> independent review -> close. A person must not independently close their own work. Declines and escalations must be recorded.
7. An institutional proposal is a reviewed agenda candidate, not an automatic motion or vote. Quorum, majorities, abstentions, tie handling, appeal and certification rules remain for formal ratification.
8. Conflict of interest must be disclosed; the affected member does not approve their own benefit or audit their own action. Finance separates initiation, authorization, payment, reconciliation and audit.
9. Child protection, urgent harm, protected information, serious fraud and out-of-mandate actions require stop/escalation to authorized specialists. No demo authorizes real emergency dispatch or case handling.
10. Governance is voluntary. No required donations or automatic salary. Any reimbursement or payment requires transparent, independently checked and legally reviewed policy.
11. Sensitive member/case details, actual votes, real payments and privileged credentials must not enter public GitHub Pages demos. Separate secure server and ratified rules are required before binding governance.
12. Material policy changes must be versioned, documented and submitted to council; the public static demo is not a ratification mechanism.

## B. Accessibility acceptance criteria
- Responsive mobile layout, visible focus indicators, touch targets at least 44px for the newly introduced return controls.
- Keyboard-operable navigation and explicit return links from V14-B, V14-C, V15, V11-B and V8 to the unified governance center.
- Main center includes a keyboard skip link, semantic headings, a main landmark, clear link names, readable warnings and reduced-motion support.
- Avoid communicating real security or binding voting from any synthetic page. Do not rely solely on color to explain warnings.
- Full WCAG compliance, screen-reader compatibility and device-specific accessibility have NOT been certified by this repository inspection.
