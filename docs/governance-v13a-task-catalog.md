## Non-negotiable boundaries
- 25 institutional council seats, including two co-chairs, each have exactly one council vote; no co-chair veto.
- Only the council determines institution-wide policy, constitution, consolidated budget, strategic commitments and institutional decisions.
- Operational clusters (salkımlar) independently decide delegated routine matters inside approved policy, budget, jurisdiction, safety and professional limits. They do not cast council votes.
- A council seat is accountable for coordination and reporting; it is not automatically the sole operator or unilateral approver.
- No real identities, task assignments, case data, financial transfers, product approvals, dispatch or live meeting voting in this static pilot.
- Roles and allocations below remain proposed, not approved appointments. All board participation is voluntary; no salary entitlement.
- Safeguarding, emergency intervention, minors, protected data, real commerce and financial handling require separately validated legal, professional, security and provider readiness gates.

## Minimum synthetic task record
- task_id: locally generated synthetic identifier
- responsible_seat: exactly one of 01–25
- cluster: the seat's primary cluster or a documented collaborating cluster
- activity_area: one named portal activity
- task_type: delegated_operational | institutional_proposal
- title and description: fictional, non-sensitive
- assigned_role: coordinator | specialist | digital_citizen | council_seat (role label only, no actual person)
- consulted_seats: optional seat identifiers
- status: planned | in_progress | blocked | completed
- due_date: optional illustrative date
- outcome_note: fictional, non-sensitive
- escalation_reason: optional, only for institutional proposal or safety/policy/budget boundary
- audit_label: SYNTHETIC / LOCAL / NON-BINDING

## Decision-routing rule
1. If the task is routine, within a validated delegation and approved limits, it stays in its operational cluster; its outcome may be reported to the responsible seat.
2. If it changes institution-wide policy, constitution, consolidated budget or strategic commitment, create a council proposal; never auto-cast a vote.
3. If it raises safeguarding, legal, privacy, security, financial or emergency risk, pause the affected operation and route to the designated qualified review path. Do not substitute an automatic council vote for immediate professional safety protocols.
4. Invited cluster representatives can report and speak by invitation but cannot vote on council motions.
5. The meeting/video provider's moderator role is independent of governance vote rights.

## Separation of duties
- The task requester should not be the only reviewer of their own sensitive or financial transaction.
- Finance custody, payment approval, reconciliation and audit should not be collapsed into one role.
- Security/ethics complaints concerning a council member need a conflict-free review and appeal route.
- The responsible council seat coordinates accountability without acquiring unbounded operational privileges.
