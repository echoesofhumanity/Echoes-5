# Echoes Academy — Lesson 01 instructional blueprint (DRAFT)

**Program:** Conscious Parenting & Child Safeguarding  
**Module:** 1 — Safeguarding principles  
**Lesson ID:** `conscious-parenting-child-safeguarding-lesson-01`  
**Status:** Unpublished working draft. Not an approved training, legal protocol, or qualification.

## Purpose and boundaries
Equip parents, caregivers and non-specialist adults to recognize potential safeguarding concerns, respond with dignity, and seek qualified help without investigating or making diagnoses. Child safety and privacy take precedence over publicity, documentation, or the platform's reputation. No identifiable child cases are used in public teaching.

## Intended learning outcomes
1. Recognize that a concern may arise from a disclosure, a pattern of behavior, or an unsafe situation; no single sign proves abuse.
2. Demonstrate calm, non-leading listening, appropriate reassurance, and immediate safety prioritization.
3. Distinguish a supportive adult's role from that of trained safeguarding professionals and relevant authorities.

These operational outcomes elaborate the three existing program-level outcomes in `academy-program.html`; they are proposed, not yet approved.

## Narration principles
- Warm, calm and clear. Never sensationalize, dramatize or imply that a child must prove their experience.
- Do not ask learners to share personal experiences or identifiable child information.
- Do not claim that a checklist can confirm abuse.
- Do not recommend confronting a suspected abuser or conducting an interview.
- Do not promise confidentiality that safeguarding duties may prevent.
- Reporting pathways and legal obligations must be reviewed per jurisdiction before publication.

## Proposed learning check (formative, not a certificate)
**Scenario A:** A child tells an adult something worrying. Which initial response is safest?
- A. Ask detailed questions to determine exactly what happened.
- B. Listen calmly, avoid leading questions, prioritize safety and seek appropriate professional guidance. **[Intended answer]**
- C. Promise never to tell anyone.

**Scenario B:** An adult notices a single change in a child's behavior. What is the appropriate interpretation?
- A. It proves abuse.
- B. It is irrelevant.
- C. It may merit attention in context, but is not proof on its own. **[Intended answer]**

**Scenario C:** A child appears to be in immediate danger. What takes priority?
- A. Waiting for the next seminar.
- B. Contacting local emergency assistance and taking safe protective action appropriate to the situation. **[Intended answer]**
- C. Posting the situation online.

These questions are provisional and require safeguarding expert review, localization and accessibility checks.
